Indonesia BPJS & PPh 21 Payroll

Complete localization for Indonesian companies on Odoo 20

Automatically calculate BPJS contributions and PPh 21 income tax using the official TER method. Designed for Indonesian businesses that need accurate, compliant payroll calculations.

Key Features

BPJS Calculation

  • JHT (Old Age Savings) – Employee & Employer
  • JP (Pension) – with annual salary cap
  • JKK (Work Accident) – by risk class
  • JKM (Death Benefit)
  • BPJS Kesehatan – with salary cap

PPh 21 TER

  • Monthly TER calculation (Jan–Nov)
  • Full tables for Categories A, B, and C
  • Based on employee PTKP status
  • Configurable rates via Settings

Easy Configuration

Update BPJS rates, salary caps, and JKK risk class percentages directly from Settings without touching code.

Important Notes

  • Always verify rates and caps against the latest regulations (PP, PMK, Perpres).
  • December annual reconciliation uses progressive rates (not fully automated yet).
  • Compatible with Odoo 20 Community. Works with common Community payroll modules.
  • Test thoroughly before production use.
